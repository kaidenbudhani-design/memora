Before you play: gameplay data notice

Memora records your game scores, completed levels, correct and incorrect answers, and approximate response times on this device starting with Level 1. A random game nickname helps organize your progress. No real name, email, or medical information is requested.

For now, detailed gameplay records stay in your browser and are not sent to the research database. With your express opt-in, optional cloud saving sends your random nickname, age range, XP, streaks, completed levels, and gameplay attempts (accuracy and response times) if you choose to enable it later. Memora is an educational game, not a medical test or treatment.

Choose whether to share gameplay data online. You can change your mind later in Privacy settings. Declining will not stop you playing.

Privacy settings

You can request removal of your cloud records. The deletion request button stops uploads immediately and gives you an account identifier for your request; it does not automatically delete cloud records.
