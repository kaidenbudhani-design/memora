Memora: privacy & research

What the game stores now

Memora stores a randomly generated nickname, selected age range, XP, streaks, completed levels, and individual gameplay attempts (world, level, accuracy, approximate response time, and date/time) in your browser. By default, these details stay on your device and are not sent to the research database. If you choose optional anonymous cloud saving, the nickname, age range, progress, and gameplay attempts are sent to Supabase and linked through a random private account ID. The age range and nickname are stored once in the player profile; gameplay attempts link to that profile. Cloud saving is not research enrollment.

You can remove local data by clearing this site's browser data. Clearing browser data does not delete records previously synced to the cloud; cloud deletion controls are not yet available in the game.

Research participation

Research data collection is currently disabled. Playing Memora does not enroll you in a study. A future optional study will require a clear explanation and separate, voluntary consent. Declining must not prevent you from playing.

No medical diagnosis or brain-injury information is requested in this version of the game. Any future sensitive questions would be optional, separately consented to, and carefully protected.

About Memora

Memora is an educational memory game and research prototype, not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment. Research participation safeguards will be reviewed before real participant data is collected, especially for younger players.
