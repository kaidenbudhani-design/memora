Memora: privacy & research

What the game stores now

The current Memora game stores XP, streaks, and completed levels in your browser's local storage. This information stays on your device. It is not transmitted to the Memora research database.

You can remove this local progress by clearing site data in your browser.

Research participation

Research data collection is currently disabled. Playing Memora does not enroll you in a study. A future optional study will require a clear explanation and separate, voluntary consent. Declining must not prevent you from playing.

No medical diagnosis or brain-injury information is requested in this version of the game. Any future sensitive questions would be optional, separately consented to, and carefully protected.

About Memora

Memora is an educational memory game and research prototype, not a medical device, diagnosis, or treatment. Research participation safeguards will be reviewed before real participant data is collected, especially for younger players.
